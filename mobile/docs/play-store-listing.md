## App content (Policy → App content)

**Privacy policy:** the URL above.

**Ads:** No, the app doesn't contain ads.

**App access:** *All or some functionality is restricted.* Most of the app works with **"Try it without an account"** on the sign-in screen. Friends needs an account: create a test account on the website (email and password), add a friend to it if you can, and give its email and password in the instructions field. Don't use your personal account.

**Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** category *All other app types*. No violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling or purchases. Users can interact: yes, limited (friends see names, usernames and workout summaries, and send preset cheers; no free chat, photos or location). Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

**Target audience:** 16–17 and 18 and over. Not designed for children; the app checks age 16+ at sign-up.

**News app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** None.

**Health apps:** declare *Activity and fitness tracking* (workouts, routines). Body weight and measurements are optional and private by default.

**Data safety:**

- Collects or shares data: **Yes**. Encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **Yes** (in the app, on the web, and at the account deletion URL).
- Shared with third parties: **No**. (Firebase acts as a service provider; what friends see is chosen by the user.)

| Data type | Collected | Optional? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name | Yes | Optional (only with an account) | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | Optional (only with an account) | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs (@username) | Yes | Optional | App functionality, Account management |
| Health and fitness → Fitness info (routines, workouts, sets) | Yes | Optional (local mode keeps it on the phone) | App functionality |
| Health and fitness → Health info (weight, measurements) | Yes | Optional | App functionality |
| App activity → Other user-generated content (friends, cheers, onboarding answers) | Yes | Optional | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, files, messages, financial info, device IDs, advertising data, analytics or crash logs.
